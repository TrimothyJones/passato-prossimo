## passare: accepted context section

**to pass / go by**

**Context matters**

Passare is a flexible verb whose meaning changes with context. Its core idea often involves passing, moving, or changing from one state/place to another. Depending on the sentence, it can mean things like to pass by, spend (time), hand/pass something, switch/change, or go through.

Context note by Project user (this conversation); status: accepted; runtime status: local-word-lens. [Sense sanity check](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/passare/). Keep separate from individual source senses and sentence-specific interpretation.

## divenire: clarification resolved-user-accepted

Divenire can describe a change of condition, often progressive, and overlaps with diventare. Philosophical il divenire is a separate noun use. A dramatic interpretation depends on context; no rule excludes concrete transformations.

[Reference](https://dizionario.internazionale.it/parola/divenire)


## rendere: clarification resolved-user-accepted

Restitution: give back. Resultative construction: rendere + object + adjective, cause the described state. This can apply to people or things. Yield, express, and other source senses remain separate.

[Reference](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/rendere/)

- Questa notizia mi rende felice. → This news makes me happy.
- La cottura rende la pizza croccante. → Baking makes the pizza crisp.
- Faccio una pizza. → I am making a pizza.

## realizzare: clarification resolved-user-accepted

The awareness meaning is a semantic calque of English through French, documented in discussion by 1938 and accepted in the cited Crusca analysis. Attach its origin note to that sense alone; do not mark the whole verb as new or nonstandard.

[Reference](https://accademiadellacrusca.it/it/consulenza/abbiamo-realizzato-che-si-puo-usare/1241)

- Abbiamo realizzato il progetto. → We carried out the project.
- Ho realizzato che mancava una pagina. → I realized that a page was missing.
