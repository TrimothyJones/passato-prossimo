## cercare: accepted context section

**to look for; to try to (with di + infinitive)**

**Two common constructions**

Cercare + a person or thing means to look for them: cerco le chiavi — I am looking for the keys. Cercare di + infinitive means to try to do something: cerco di capire — I am trying to understand.

Context note by Codex, following user-requested construction clarification; status: accepted; runtime status: local-word-lens. [Sense sanity check](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/cercare/). Keep separate from individual source senses and sentence-specific interpretation.

## stare: clarification resolved-user-accepted

Sto bene describes wellbeing; sto a casa describes being/staying at home; sto mangiando uses stare + gerund for an action in progress. Temporary state is not a general rule for choosing stare over essere: sono stanco also describes a temporary state. Keep constructions distinct.

[Reference](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/stare/)
